# Research Brief: Chatbot for Gekko Company Website

## Problem Analysis

### Problem Statement
Customers of Gekko rely on phone and email interactions as their primary means of initial contact, with response times often outside standard business hours during peak demand periods. Additionally, repetitive questions about hours, locations, services, and pricing have become a significant drain on staff resources.

### User Needs
- Customers seek immediate answers to frequently asked queries.
- Customers need self-service options available via the website for common inquiries.
- The chatbot should direct users to the appropriate department or contact person.
- It must be able to capture initial enquiry details, including names and company information.
- The chatbot should provide links to relevant resources, forms, or documentation.

### Success Metrics
The primary metric is addressing frequently asked questions (such as hours, locations, services, and pricing) through chatbot interactions. This reduces the workload on staff members by directing users to the right department or contacting the appropriate person without further calls being made.

## Current Landscape

### Prior Art
No existing solution exists that meets all of Gekko's requirements for addressing frequently asked questions via a chatbot. There is no established self-service mechanism available through the website for common enquiries.

### Constraints
- This project has limited scope, with no timeline or specific technical stack constraints.
- No integration solutions are required as this project focuses on functionality within the existing system without involving external systems.

## Technical Considerations

### Integration Strategy
Since there is no external integration solution requirement, the chatbot can be implemented using the company's current IT infrastructure. The chatbot will likely need to integrate with various departments' internal systems and databases for accurate information retrieval and department redirection.

### User Interface Design
The chatbot should have a user-friendly interface that allows users to easily navigate through frequently asked questions without encountering errors or confusing prompts.

## Recommended Approach

Given the current landscape, implementing an existing solution if possible is recommended. If such a solution exists, it will significantly enhance customer service by reducing response times and allowing for self-service options. However, if no suitable solution exists, developing a custom chatbot can still be feasible with appropriate user interface design considerations.

## Risks & Open Questions

### Risks
- Ensuring the existing solution is available and compatible with Gekko's current systems.
- Integrating with internal databases to provide accurate information.
- Ensuring seamless navigation through frequently asked questions without errors or confusion.

### Open Questions
- Determining how frequently the chatbot needs to address common enquiries, potentially impacting its frequency of updates.
- Considering additional training for staff on using the chatbot effectively.
- Evaluating the long-term impact of implementing a chatbot in terms of reduced workload and improved customer satisfaction.
